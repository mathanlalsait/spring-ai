HR Policy Summary (sample training document)

Leave policy:
- Employees receive 18 days of paid leave annually.
- Up to 8 unused leave days can be carried over to the next year.
- Maternity leave is 6 months. Paternity leave is 2 weeks.

Working hours:
- Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.
- National holidays are company-wide off days.

Notice period:
- The standard notice period is 30 days.

Benefits:
- Benefits include health insurance, provident fund, and an annual health checkup.

Code of conduct:
- Employees must follow professional behavior, punctuality, and data confidentiality.
